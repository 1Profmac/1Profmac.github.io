## Lesson body (paste in this order)

### 1. Opening

You asked your first question. You got your first answer. That is already more than most people ever do.

This lesson is the follow-up. Most beginners read the first answer and close the tab. The real value lives in what happens next.

### 2. Video

Embed the HeyGen / Barb clip recorded from `lessons/lesson-02c/script.txt` (~4–5 minutes).

If the video is not ready yet, keep the practice below live. The practice *is* the lesson.

### 3. The idea

Think about a good doctor or a good teacher. You did not ask one question and leave. You said, wait, say more about that.

AI works the same way. You do not start over. In the same conversation, it remembers what you already said.

### 4. Practice (required)

Open the same ChatGPT tab you used in Lesson 2b. Stay logged out if you do not have an account. Copy **one** line. Paste it. Press Enter.

- Can you explain that in simpler terms?
- Can you give me a real example of how someone my age would use this?
- What should I do first?
- I did not understand the part about ________.
- Can you make that shorter?
- What should I check with a real person before I use this?

One follow-up is enough. Two is even better.

**Catch-up if they skipped 2b.** Paste this first, then come back to a follow-up:

> I am over 50 and new to AI. I want to use it for something practical in my life. Explain the most useful thing I can do with it today, in five short steps, in plain language.

Button / link: `https://chatgpt.com` (new tab). Do **not** connect the ChatGPT API.

### 5. Stay in charge

It is extra help. It is not a replacement for your doctor, your family, or a real friend.

Stop if the answer is about medicine, money advice, or the law — check with a real person.

Never paste: Social Security number, bank numbers, passwords, phone codes.

If someone you do not know asks you to send money or a code, stop. That is a scam. Lesson 3 covers that.

### 6. Close

You are not behind. You have not missed your window. You are a Digital Pioneer, and today you proved it.

Stay in ChatGPT a little longer. Ask something that matters to you.
