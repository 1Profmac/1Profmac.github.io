## Long wrap (3:30–5:00)

Look at what you refused to believe about yourself an hour ago — or three lessons ago.

You are not “bad at tech.” You had a bad setup: too much speed, too little respect, and a culture that treats age like a punchline.

AARP says most adults our age already carry a smartphone. Thirty percent have already tried the new kind of AI. You are not a museum exhibit. You are late to a party that was badly hosted.

You now have a tool habit: one question, one useful answer, no private numbers in the box.

You now have a safety habit: costumes, pressure, a long password, a second lock, a family word, two government websites if something already went wrong.
