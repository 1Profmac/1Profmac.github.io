## Script

This is Lesson 2. The job is small on purpose.

Open **one** tool. Ask **one** real question. Leave with **one** useful answer.

`[ON SCREEN]`  
One tool. One real question. One useful answer.

I use ChatGPT’s free site — chatgpt.com. If Copilot is already on your Windows PC, that is fine. Pick one. Do not tour the mall.

Before you type, the rule:

`[ON SCREEN]`  
Never paste  
SSN · bank numbers · passwords · phone codes

AI is not a person. It is a fast tool that writes a reply. It can help. It can also be confidently wrong. You stay in charge. Health, money, and the law get a human check.

A lazy question looks like this: “dinners.”

A real question looks like this:

> I am 68. I cook for two. I have chicken, rice, and spinach. Give me three easy dinners. No spicy food. List extra ingredients and short steps.

`[ON SCREEN]`  
Situation + Ask + Format

Who you are. What you need. What the answer should look like.

If the reply is useful, you will understand it and you could do the next step.

If it is too long, you do not quit. You say, “Make that simpler.” That follow-up *is* the skill.

`[ON SCREEN]`  
Follow-up  
“Make that simpler.”  
“Give me three options.”

Do it now, on a question from your real week — a letter, a leftover, a paragraph you do not understand. Screenshot the good answer.

Then come to Lesson 3. Same week you learn to ask, you learn how not to get rushed by a fake.

I’m Brian McKinney. Go ask your question.
