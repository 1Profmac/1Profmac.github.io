# Lesson 1 — Welcome

**Version:** Short  
**Runtime:** 3:00–3:45  
**On-screen title:** Welcome. You are not too late.  
**Fridge line:** You are not too late. You just needed the right teacher.

**Use:** QR landing, email drip, social, live-session open.

---

## Script

Hi. I’m Brian McKinney. I’m 65. I run my company with AI — and I teach adults 50 and older to use the same tools.

If you have ever thought, “I missed it,” I want thirty seconds of your time.

You did not miss it.

`[ON SCREEN]`  
You’re not bad at technology.  
You’ve just never had the right teacher.

AARP says nine out of ten adults over 50 already own a smartphone. You are not starting from zero.

The new piece is this: you can type a real question into a tool and get a useful answer. In one year, the share of people our age doing that nearly doubled. Most of them still call themselves beginners. So do I, on a new app. Beginner is allowed.

This free series is three lessons. That is all.

`[ON SCREEN]`  
1  Welcome — you belong here  
2  Talk to AI — one question, one useful answer  
3  Don’t get scammed — fake, pressure, passwords, 2FA

No coding. No jargon. No one grabbing the phone out of your hand.

Lesson 2, we open **one** tool and ask **one** question from your real life. Dinner. A letter. A paragraph you do not understand. You leave with something you can use.

Lesson 3, we get practical about scams — how to spot a fake, how pressure works, and how to lock the door with a better password and a second step called 2FA. We will not terrify you. We will make you harder to rush.

I will never ask for your Social Security number, your bank login, or a code from your phone. If someone who *says* they are us does that — hang up. That is not us.

`[ON SCREEN]`  
Do this now  
Write one real question you want help with this week.

Write one real question on a sticky note. Bring it to Lesson 2.

You’re not too late. You just needed the right teacher.

I’m Brian. I’ll see you in the next lesson.

`[ON SCREEN]`
