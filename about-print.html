We exist so adults 50+ are never left behind.

Purpose

Learn More Technologies exists so adults 50 and older can live, work, create, and connect in a digital world without being left behind. We exist to prove that age is not a barrier to technology — it is an asset — and that digital independence is a right, not a privilege reserved for the already fluent.

Mission

Our mission is to close the digital divide for adults 50+ through free, hands-on AI and technology education delivered in community — so overlooked talent can create, stay safe online, remain employable, and participate fully in civic and cultural life. We operate 50+TechBridge as that mission in practice: live workshops in libraries, senior centers, and cultural sites; bilingual English and Spanish materials; and a creative-output model grounded in dignity, accessibility, and community.

One sentence

Learn More Technologies exists to make digital independence a right for adults 50+ — not a privilege for the already fluent.

Dignity

No participant should feel embarrassed for not knowing. Instruction respects adult intelligence — never a stereotype of age.

Accessibility

Free programming in English and Spanish, at neighborhood places people already trust. Cost and jargon should not be the barrier.

Community

Technology education is strongest together. Lived experience is an asset AI can amplify — not replace.

Organization: Learn More Technologies LLC (dba 50+TechBridge) is an Austin, Texas education organization — MBE-certified, WIOA-eligible, and a City of Austin Nexus Grant recipient — dedicated to AI literacy and digital skills for adults 50+.
