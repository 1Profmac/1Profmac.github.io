About Brian

Brian McKinney is the founder of 50+TechBridge — the only program in America teaching AI skills specifically to adults 50+. A solo CEO who manages 30+ AI-powered workflows, Brian is living proof that age is no barrier to innovation. His free AI training program is deployed across 13 locations in Central Texas with a targeted 3x completion rate. He publishes Agentic50 Weekly and speaks on ageism, AI adoption, and the $76 trillion opportunity the market is ignoring.

Speaking Topics

Keynote 01

"The $76 Trillion Mistake: Why Ignoring Adults 50+ Is Your Biggest Business Risk"

The 50+ market controls $76 trillion in global wealth and represents the fastest-growing workforce segment — yet most organizations treat them as an afterthought. Brian makes the economic case for inclusion and delivers a framework for reversing the trend before the opportunity closes.

Best for: Corporate executives, workforce boards, economic development, chambers of commerce, HR & DEI conferences
Keynote 02

"Ageism Is the Last Acceptable Bias — And AI Is Making It Worse"

Ageism costs the U.S. economy $850 billion a year. Now AI is accelerating it — automating away jobs held by experienced workers while creating new ones nobody is training older adults to fill. Brian delivers a clear-eyed look at the crisis and the path forward.

Best for: Policy conferences, aging services, nonprofits, foundations, workforce development, public libraries, government
Workshop / Lunch & Learn
