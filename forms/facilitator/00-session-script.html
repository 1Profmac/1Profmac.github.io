Session Script
110 minutes
MinBlockWhat you doDone
0–10Arrive and settleNames. “What worked since last week?” Devices on. Font size. Pass sign-in now.
10–20One story, one ruleWhy this matters at 50+. One rule for the hour. No theory dump.
20–35I doDemo slowly on the big screen. Same steps they will use.
35–60We doClass does those steps together. Wait. Repeat. Do not add a second app.
60–90You doEach person completes the win on their own phone or laptop.
90–100Save the winThey copy the steps onto the take-home card.
100–110Confidence check3-question exit slip. Next week preview. Confirm return date.
Always
  • One AI tool per cohort
  • Their device, not a lab PC if possible
  • Same 5 steps, three times
  • Shame-free language — never “even grandma”
  • 3 photos + verbal OK (see instructor package)
Never
  • Ten apps in one day
  • How LLMs work
  • Medical or legal advice from the AI
  • Crypto, gold, or side hustles
  • Skipping the sign-in sheet
Exit slip (say these out loud)
  1. On a scale of 1–5, how confident are you doing today’s task alone?
  2. What is one thing you will do again this week?
  3. What still feels unclear?
