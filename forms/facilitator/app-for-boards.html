The Lesson 2 App — what boards can include and refer

A documented instructional coach for Digital Pioneer Module 1 (Talk to AI). Barb teaches. ChatGPT is the public tool. #AGENTIC50 is the earned mark after the first win. Boards buy the program and the proof — not a senior chatbot.

What this is (one sentence)

The app’s job is to make the first AI practice the same at every site and leave a named, dated completion record a workforce board can file with an IEP/ISS, ETPL description, or partner referral. It started as “Talk to Barb” — a destination chatbot — because that is what a first app looks like. That is the wrong product for monetization and for reviewers. Organizations pay for access, proof, and delivery. Learners get a free start. The app is how Lesson 2 is delivered and documented. It is not the invoice.

Copy this into an ETPL / program description

Introductory AI literacy is practiced in a facilitated Lesson 2 coach (Barb) that opens a public workplace tool rather than a proprietary chatbot. Adults 50+ complete one documented prompt and one human-check follow-up. The program issues a named completion record and a pre/post confidence survey. Full Digital Pioneer Core is 6 weeks / 12 hours with a Week 5 fork for job search or workplace tools.

“This or something better” — the standard you can refer

Boards may adopt the 50+TechBridge Lesson 2 coach, or an equivalent that meets all four tests. If a vendor cannot meet them, it is not a substitute.

  1. Teaches a public AI tool the participant can reopen at work or at home — not only a branded wrapper.
  2. Requires a human-check follow-up (critical evaluation), not a single generated answer.
  3. Produces a named, dated completion record the board can file.
  4. Is designed for adults 50+ (large type, stay-logged-out path, no account required to start, no health-bot first screen).

Referral sentence: “Use the 50+TechBridge Lesson 2 coach, or another tool that meets the four-test AI-literacy practice standard in the 50+TechBridge board packet.” Other boards can include that sentence. LMT is the reference implementation.
