Week 1 Guide

End state: Every person asked one real question in one AI tool and got a useful answer. If they can do that, Week 1 succeeded. Device setup is only the first 10 minutes — not the lesson.

Before you walk in
  • Pick one tool for the whole cohort (ChatGPT, Gemini, or Copilot — not all three).
  • Create or confirm a classroom account if Wi‑Fi login will be a barrier.
  • Print: sign-in, 3–5 photo releases, take-home cards, pre-survey (first session only).
  • Write 3 demo questions on a sticky: a recipe or letter, a trip, a work/life task.
Room plan
BlockSay / do
Welcome + device (10)Pre-survey. Goal: family / health / job / work. Font size. Open the browser. “We are not learning the phone today. We are getting an answer.”
One story, one rule (10)Story: it’s not too late. Rule: the AI can be wrong — you decide if the answer is useful.
I do (15)Open the tool. Type: “I’m 62 and new to this. Explain [simple thing] in 5 short steps.” Read the answer out loud. Change one word and show it update.
We do (25)Everyone types the same starter prompt together. Wait for every screen. Help one person at a time. Do not introduce a second tool.
You do (30)They ask their question (letter to family, job task, recipe, appointment). Facilitator circulates. Win = they can read the answer back.
Follow-up · 2c (in the room or take-home)Same tab. One more line: “Make that shorter” or “What should I check with a real person?” Print lesson-02c-practice.html. Learner page: lessons/lesson-02c-follow-up.html.
Save + exit (20)Fill the take-home card. Exit slip. Next week: scams and fake voices. Confirm date.
Starter prompt (write on the board)

I’m new to AI. I’m over 50. Explain ________ in 5 short steps. Use plain language. Do not give medical or legal advice.

If someone is stuck
  • No email yet → pair them, or use the classroom account.
  • Tiny text → Settings → Display → largest font. Then come back to the tool.
  • Afraid to type → they dictate, you type the first line, they do the rest.
  • Wants a different app → “One tool this week. You can try another after class.”

Do not teach how the model works, compare five chatbots, or let Week 1 become a smartphone class. If they leave with one useful answer, you are on brand and you can sell this as a 90-minute pilot.
