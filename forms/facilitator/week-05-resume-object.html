Resume object

What this is: a small JSON file of facts the learner owns, plus optional AI wording they edited. It is the ground truth for resume / interview practice. It is not a chatbot and not a seventh week.

Lab rule: AI drafts a resume bullet or interview answer. They edit it. They do not paste a lie. Nothing in the file is treated as true until attestedTrue is checked by the learner.

The model (four layers)

Room plan (You do)
  1. Open resume-object/index.html. Load blank, or the Pat Rivera sample to show the shape.
  2. Facts first. If they have no duty lines, stop. Do not let the tool invent a career.
  3. Copy the generated ask. Open ChatGPT (or the cohort’s one tool) in a new tab. No API. No iframe.
  4. Paste the draft back. They rewrite it. They may check “I edited this. It is true.” only after the wording changed.
  5. Download JSON. Optional: attach filename to a later completion slip / IEP note. Do not staple a ChatGPT printout.

Do not
  • Do not paste WIOA, ETPL, $18K, or IEP jargon onto the learner screens.
  • Do not add an OpenAI key, a chat widget, or an iframe of ChatGPT.
  • Do not treat an AI bullet as evidence of employment.
  • Do not invent a seventh curriculum week. This sits inside Week 5 fork B.
  • Do not collect SSN, bank routing, passwords, or medical diagnoses in this file.

If the tool writes “increased sales 40%” and they never counted sales — that is a lie. Delete it. The win is an honest sentence they can say in an interview.
