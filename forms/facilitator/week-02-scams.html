Week 2 Guide

End state: They can name one pressure tactic, turn on 2FA or write a password plan, and know to hang up / not send money when a “family” voice asks.

Reuse from the old course

Phishing examples, password basics, “pause before you click.” Do not throw those slides away. Relabel the week.

Add (this is the new week)
  • Fake grandchild / relative voice (AI voice clone)
  • Fake photo or invoice
  • Someone asking for a code, gift cards, or wire
You do

Review one real-looking suspicious email or text together. Then each person: (1) turn on 2FA or write where their passwords will live, and (2) write the family rule: “We will call back on a known number before sending money.”

Take-home: “Our family callback rule” + one screenshot of 2FA on, or a written password plan. Use the session script.
