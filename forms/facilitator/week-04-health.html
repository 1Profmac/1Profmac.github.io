End state: They open a portal or write 3 questions for a visit. They know AI is not their doctor.

Reuse

Patient portal, refill, telehealth join-link from the old course.

Add

AI prompt: “Help me write 3 questions to ask my doctor about ________. Do not diagnose. Do not suggest I skip a visit.” Then the rule: verify with a clinician.

Stop box: If the AI names a diagnosis, dose, or “you don’t need to see a doctor,” discard it. Write that on the take-home.
