5. Government and minority contract path
Housekeeping (unlock everything else)
  1. SAM.gov registration + UEI active and renewed.
  2. One-page capability statement: NAICS 611420 / 611430 / 624310, MBE TX, past performance, key personnel, price range.
  3. ETPL listing with your local workforce board (or letter of intent from board).
  4. One unified price list that falls inside standard ITA ranges.
  5. Named facilitator (even one certified person) so you can say yes without being the instructor.
First contracts to chase (Texas, you can win)
  • Workforce Solutions boards — WIOA Title I Adult / Dislocated Worker; ITA or cohort contract; scope includes one leadership briefing.
  • City of Austin / Travis County — aging, digital equity, HUB set-aside procurement.
  • Austin Public Library system contract (not just a room-use agreement).
  • Community college adult ed WIOA Title II — enter as subcontractor first.
Write these into every SOW
  • One staff / board briefing (or keynote) — this is Brian on stage, paid by the contract.
  • Outcomes report and pre/post survey data — this builds past performance.
  • Optional AGENTIC50 episode with consented participants — this is the podcast story.
  • Digital Pioneer Gallery Walk if cohort is 6-week community track — this is the arts/inclusion evidence.
Second wave (funds the speaker strategy)
  • State digital-equity / broadband digital skills (NTIA-funded state plans) — partner; do not prime a large grant alone yet.
  • ACL / area agencies on aging — AI + independence / scam prevention (your Week 2).
  • DOL TEGL 03-25 lane — AI literacy explicitly authorized; cite the article already on LMT.com.
  • Corporate DEI / workforce foundation grants (AARP Foundation, JPMorgan WorkForce, Google.org).

Do not chase: Giant federal primes with no past-performance packet. GSA Schedule before two clean local contracts. Grants that require Brian to teach every session. Anything that funds "podcasting" as the deliverable — fund training, treat the show as dissemination.
