I am writing on behalf of [Organization Name] to express our enthusiastic support for the 50+TechBridge program operated by Learn More Technologies (LMT), and to confirm our ongoing partnership with this organization.

[Organization Name] has hosted 50+TechBridge Technology Assisted Training sessions at our location since [Month, Year]. During this time, we have welcomed [#] sessions serving approximately [#] adults age 50 and older from our community.

50+TechBridge fills a critical gap in the services available to our community members. Adults 50+ who participate in these sessions gain practical artificial intelligence skills that help them stay employed, navigate healthcare systems, and participate more fully in daily life. The program is delivered free of charge, on-site, and with no burden to our organization — LMT provides all materials, instruction, and technology.

We have observed firsthand the enthusiasm and growth of participants who previously described themselves as "not tech people." The program's approach — hands-on, jargon-free, and customized to our community — has made it one of the most well-attended programs we have hosted for this age group.

We strongly support LMT's application to the AACME Elevate program and believe that this funding will allow 50+TechBridge to reach more adults across Austin — including communities that have historically been underserved by arts and technology programming. We look forward to continuing and expanding our partnership.
