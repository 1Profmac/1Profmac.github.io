Session Checklists

📋 Before the Session

  • Print sign-in sheets (1 per 20 participants)
  • Print testimonial release forms (3–5 per session)
  • Confirm room/tech setup with site contact
  • Test WiFi and projector on arrival
  • Bring printed flyers for participants to take home
  • Charge all devices
  • Have your phone ready to take 3+ session photos

✅ During the Session

  • Pass sign-in sheet at the START — before people leave early
  • Take at least 3 photos — participants engaged with devices
  • Note any participant with a great story for a testimonial
  • Ask willing participants to fill out testimonial release
  • Confirm next session date with attendees
  • Collect any paper materials before leaving

📤 After the Session

  • Scan sign-in sheet — save as SIGNIN-[Location]-[Date].pdf
  • Label photos: PHOTO-[Location]-[Date]-[##].jpg
  • File all docs in ELEVATE-2026 folder
  • Complete session totals on sign-in sheet before filing
  • Email Brian any testimonials or standout participant stories
  • Update attendance count in tracking spreadsheet

📸 Photo Requirements

  • Minimum 3 photos per session
  • Show participants actively using devices — not posed
  • Include the room/location in at least 1 photo
  • Get verbal OK before photographing anyone's face
  • If someone declines, photograph from behind or angle away
  • Good lighting — near a window if possible

Program Standards

What We Always Do

  • Pass the sign-in sheet at the START of every session
  • Take at least 3 photos per session
  • Get signed releases before using anyone's photo or quote
  • File all documentation within 24 hours of session
  • Treat every participant with dignity and patience
  • Meet participants where they are — no judgment on skill level

What We Never Do

  • Inflate attendance numbers — use only what's on the sign-in sheet
  • Photograph participants without verbal permission
  • Use participant names or quotes without a signed release
  • Promise participants anything not approved by Brian
  • Leave sessions without collecting the sign-in sheet
  • Use jargon or make participants feel behind

Questions? Contact Brian directly.

Do not wait until the next session if something is unclear.
