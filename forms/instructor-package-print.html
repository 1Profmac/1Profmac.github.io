Session Checklists

Before the Session

  • Print sign-in sheet (1 per 20 participants)
  • Print testimonial releases (3–5 per session)
  • Confirm room and tech setup with site contact
  • Test WiFi and projector on arrival
  • Bring printed flyers for participants
  • Charge all devices
  • Phone ready to take 3+ session photos

During the Session

  • Pass sign-in sheet at START — before people leave
  • Take at least 3 photos — participants with devices
  • Note participants with great stories for testimonial
  • Ask willing participants to fill out release form
  • Confirm next session date with attendees
  • Collect all paper materials before leaving

After the Session

  • Scan sign-in sheet — save as SIGNIN-[Location]-[Date].pdf
  • Label photos: PHOTO-[Location]-[Date]-[##].jpg
  • File all docs in ELEVATE-2026 folder within 24 hrs
  • Complete session totals on sign-in sheet first
  • Email Brian any testimonials or standout stories
  • Update attendance count in tracking spreadsheet

Photo Requirements

  • Minimum 3 photos per session — no exceptions
  • Show participants actively using devices — not posed
  • Include the room/location in at least 1 photo
  • Get verbal OK before photographing anyone's face
  • If declined — photograph from behind or angle away
  • Shoot near a window for good natural light

Quick Session Report — Complete After Every Session
Program Standards

What We Always Do

  • Pass sign-in sheet at the START of every session
  • Take at least 3 photos per session
  • Get signed releases before using quotes or photos
  • File all documentation within 24 hours
  • Treat every participant with dignity and patience
  • Meet participants where they are — no judgment

What We Never Do

  • Inflate attendance — use only sign-in sheet numbers
  • Photograph participants without verbal permission
  • Use names or quotes without a signed release
  • Promise anything not approved by Brian
  • Leave without collecting the sign-in sheet
  • Use jargon or make participants feel behind
