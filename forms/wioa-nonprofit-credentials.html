What it takes to operate 50+TechBridge as a WIOA-qualified nonprofit

WIOA-eligible on a brochure is not the same as being on a state’s Eligible Training Provider List (ETPL). 501(c)(3) status is not the same as a career-school license. Texas MBE is a for-profit credential. The working model is two entities: keep Learn More Technologies LLC (MBE + invoices) and add a Texas nonprofit sidecar (grants + free seats). Do not convert the LLC.

2. Recommended legal structure — do this, not a conversion

Entity A — keep. Learn More Technologies LLC dba 50+TechBridge. For-profit. Holds Texas MBE. Invoices employers, libraries, boards, and keynotes. Signs site-license and facilitator contracts. Applies to Texas ETPL as the training provider unless counsel puts the school license on the nonprofit. Pick one provider of record.

Entity B — add. New Texas nonprofit corporation (distinct legal name, e.g. 50+TechBridge Foundation / Digital Pioneer Institute — lawyer confirms). Files IRS Form 1023. Receives grants. Buys free seats from the LLC or scholarships learners. Never the MBE vehicle.

Name collision. The LLC already uses 50+TechBridge as a DBA. Do not put the identical assumed name on the charity without counsel. One brand, two legal names, a written license/services agreement. If the IRS letter is slow, a fiscal sponsor can receive grants now — the sponsor is the legal grantee.

B. Career school license — the Texas ETPL gate

Texas Education Code Chapter 132: almost all career training requires a TWC Certificate of Approval unless TWC grants a written exemption. A 501(c)(3) letter is evidence for an exemption request; it is not the exemption. Boards will not send you to statewide ETPL without the license or the TWC exemption letter. TWC Career Schools; 40 TAC §840.10

C. WIOA / ETPL in Texas
  1. Call Workforce Solutions Capital Area (Austin local board) as the first board of record.
  2. Board issues EDvera access. Upload provider, campus, and program files.
  3. Attach TWC Certificate of Approval or TWC exemption letter.
  4. Map each paid program to a SOC / target occupation. Attach an employer support letter or employer advisory committee.
  5. Submit the Student Data Report (all students, not only WIOA). New programs: file community-cohort data and say the course is new where data are thin.
  6. Local board recommends → TWC statewide ETPL. Listing ≠ referrals. ITA caps are local (often roughly $7k–$9k — confirm with Capital Area before you print a price).
D. Texas extras people skip
  • Workers’ compensation — optional for many private Texas employers; venues, boards, and grants will require a certificate if you have any employee.
  • GL + additional insured — libraries and senior centers require COI. Add professional / educators’ liability and cyber / PII if you store WIOA data.
  • Unemployment tax — TWC UI account if you have employees. CMBL / HUB stays on the LLC. Site MOUs must match ETPL campus addresses (APL, AARP-affiliated centers, Foundation Communities).

5. Massachusetts

Do not treat Massachusetts as a brochure line. The moment you solicit donations, hire, lease, or teach in MA, clocks start. A foreign (Texas) charity must register with the MA Secretary of the Commonwealth within 10 days of commencing business and with the Attorney General before charitable work or fundraising. B. Private occupational school license (G.L. c. 112 §263)

Massachusetts licenses private post-secondary occupational schools — for-profit and nonprofit. Step 1 is always the License Determination with DOL (Office of Private Occupational School Education).

  • If DOL says you must be licensed: OSA financial / surety review → ePLACE → nonprofit fee $1,250 (for-profit $2,500) → surety bond → enrollment agreement (G.L. c. 255 §13K) → catalog / policies → instructor CORI + SORI → building and fire inspection if you have a MA facility → DOL site visit. Incomplete applications sit 9 months, then die.
  • Distance / online exemption: typically only if you have no place of business in the Commonwealth. Flying in to teach at a Boston library, senior center, or MassHire site is in-person MA delivery — get the License Determination in writing first.
  • Corporations not formed as schools may need to amend articles with SOS to include educational / school purposes.
C. MassHire ETPL (TrainingPro)
  1. Register in TrainingPro (MDCS). Provider review is state; course review is the local MassHire Workforce Board.
  2. Applications / renewals due before June 1 each year. Miss it and you wait a cycle.
  3. New providers (and Trade / Section 30) are told to include course performance data from the past two years. If the course is new, say so and start tracking immediately. Confirm current MDCS minimums (completion often ~70%) before you apply.
  4. Same occupational-alignment problem as Texas: a job, a credential, and a price — not a community AI workshop.

Practical sequence for MA: Do not open a Massachusetts site until Texas ETPL is live and one facilitator exists. Then: SOS foreign qualify → AGO charity + solicitation certificate → DOL License Determination → TrainingPro. Fundraising emails to MA donors without the AGO certificate is the fastest way to get a problem.

7. Suggested order — do not file Massachusetts first
  1. This week: Confirm SAM.gov / UEI on the LLC. Confirm the exact name on the Texas MBE certificate. Start a cohort outcomes spreadsheet from existing sign-in/surveys.
  2. Counsel call: Dual-entity plan, charity name, related-party agreement, which entity is the ETPL provider of record.
  3. Form Entity B: Texas SOS Form 202 → EIN → bylaws/policies → board of 3 → IRS Form 1023. Fiscal sponsor only if a grant cannot wait.
  4. TWC Career Schools: Exemption request (or Certificate of Approval if they say no), before promising boards a listing date.
  5. Workforce Solutions Capital Area: EDvera packet, employer letter, Student Data Report, ITA-range prices → statewide ETPL.
  6. Comptroller AP-204 after the IRS letter. Charity bank account. Seat-purchase agreement: charity buys free seats from the LLC.
  7. Massachusetts (later): SOS foreign qualify → AGO Charity Portal + Certificate of Solicitation → DOL License Determination → TrainingPro by the June 1 cycle.

Bottom line: You already have the operating company, the MBE, the footprint, and the teaching system. You do not yet have a charity, a TWC school determination, or an ETPL listing. Those three — plus SAM.gov confirmed — are the licenses that turn “WIOA-eligible” copy into money. Keep the LLC. Add the nonprofit. Do not convert.
