Platform Research Doc

90 minutes. 6 platforms. 3–5 pain phrases each. Bring these quotes back and the platform direction becomes obvious.

What You're Listening For

You're not looking for opinions. You're looking for pain phrases — the exact words 50+ adults use when they're frustrated, stuck, or wishing something existed. Phrases like: "I wish someone would just..." / "Nobody explains it like..." / "I gave up on..." / "I'd pay for..." / "I feel stupid when..."
