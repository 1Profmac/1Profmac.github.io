Building the Bridge Between
Older Adults and AI

Learn More Technologies (LMT), dba 50+TechBridge, is an Austin, Texas-based workforce development and AI education organization dedicated to closing the digital skills gap for adults age 50 and older.

LMT operates the only program in Austin delivering structured, AI-focused digital skills training specifically to adults 50+ — using a creative output model that positions technology as a tool for artistic expression, storytelling, and community participation.

Organizational Status

  • Business Entity: Learn More Technologies, LLC (dba 50+TechBridge)
  • AACME Tier: Elevate — Arts Group
  • MBE-Certified (Minority Business Enterprise)
  • WIOA-Eligible Training Provider
  • Prior City of Austin Nexus Grant — $5,000 (awarded)
  • 501(c)(3) Application in Process — 2026

How We Operate

  • Training delivered free of charge to all participants
  • Embedded inside community sites participants already use
  • Curriculum customized to each partner's population
  • Spanish-language delivery at select sites
  • Creative output model — participants make things
  • Program culminates in public Community Showcase
