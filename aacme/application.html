Mission and Artistic Vision

Creative Work — 50 points
"What is your mission or artistic vision, and what do you do to support it?"

Must cover: Your mission + the creative work supporting it  |  Who your work impacts and how they participate  |  Artistic values, traditions, or focus guiding your mission

Learn More Technologies LLC, operating as 50+TechBridge, exists to close the digital divide for adults 50+ in Austin through hands-on AI literacy education. Our mission is to ensure that every adult in our community — regardless of age, income, or prior tech experience — can confidently use AI tools to improve their quality of life, stay connected to family, and participate fully in Austin's evolving creative and cultural economy.

Our creative work takes the form of live, facilitated workshops held in Austin's public libraries and cultural centers. Each session transforms technology from something intimidating into something empowering. Participants — we call them Digital Pioneers — leave with real skills: how to use AI tools to write, research, create, manage finances, and protect themselves from digital fraud. We have served 264+ adults across 9 libraries, 3 senior centers, and 13 locations throughout Austin.

The people we impact are Austin's 50+ adult population, with emphasis on communities most affected by the digital divide: Black and Latino adults, seniors living on fixed incomes, and workers facing displacement from AI automation. Our participants don't just attend — they engage, ask questions, share their stories, and build relationships with peers navigating the same challenges. Technology education becomes a creative act of community-building.

Our work is guided by three core values: dignity (no participant should feel embarrassed for not knowing something), accessibility (free programming offered in English and Spanish at neighborhood locations), and community (technology education is most powerful when it happens together, not in isolation). We believe that the 50+ generation's lived experience, resilience, and creativity are assets — and that AI can amplify those assets rather than replace them.

Proposed Public Event

Creative Work — 50 points
"What is your proposed public event and how does it reflect the people, places, or cultures of Austin?"

Must cover: When, Where, What, How  |  How it reflects Austin's people, places, and/or cultures  |  "Authentic perspective and meaningful cultural expression"

50+TechBridge proposes a 6-session AI Literacy Workshop Series hosted at Austin Public Library branches and cultural centers across the city, running October 2026 through March 2027. Each session is free, open to the public, and designed for adults 50+ with no prior tech experience required.

WHEN: Monthly workshops, October 2026 – March 2027, held on weekday mornings and Saturday afternoons to maximize accessibility for working and retired participants.

WHERE: Austin Public Library branches serving Austin's most culturally diverse neighborhoods, and the Mexican American Cultural Center — anchor institutions that are already trusted gathering places for the communities we serve.

WHAT: 90-minute hands-on workshops teaching practical AI tools — conversational AI assistants, AI-powered writing and creativity tools, scam protection, and AI for everyday life tasks: writing letters, managing health information, staying connected with family, and building small businesses.

HOW: Small groups of 15–20 participants, facilitated by trained instructors, with printed take-home guides in English and Spanish. Every session includes time for questions, peer sharing, and practice on participant-owned devices.

This program reflects Austin authentically. We meet people in their own neighborhoods — in libraries that have served Austin communities for generations. Our curriculum centers Austin's own stories: a grandmother learning to video call her grandchildren, a retired educator writing her memoir with AI assistance, a 62-year-old worker preparing for a job market that has changed around him. These are Austin stories. This is Austin culture — resilient, community-rooted, and forward-looking.

Marketing Plan

Public Connection — 20 points
"How will you market your proposed event?"

Must cover: Methods to reach residents, visitors, and tourists  |  How you promote Austin as a cultural destination  |  How people will feel connected and encouraged to attend

50+TechBridge will market the AI Literacy Workshop Series through a multi-channel strategy reaching Austin residents across every zip code — with targeted emphasis on the neighborhoods served by our library partners.

Channels: (1) Austin Public Library network — posters, table tents, and staff referrals at all 20 Austin branches. (2) Mexican American Cultural Center — flyers and bilingual postcards distributed through their existing community networks. (3) Agentic50 email newsletter — weekly AI programming updates reaching 50+ adults and allied organizations throughout Austin. (4) LinkedIn and Facebook — organic and boosted content targeting Austin's 50+ professional and community audiences. (5) Printed 4x6 bilingual postcards — distributed at senior centers, community centers, faith communities, and healthcare waiting rooms across Austin. (6) AARP Austin chapter — program listing in their community calendar and member communications.

Promoting Austin as a cultural destination: Our workshops are hosted at Austin's most culturally significant community anchors — the very spaces that define Austin's identity as a city that honors its diverse creative traditions. Programming at these locations reinforces Austin's reputation as a place where technology and cultural legacy coexist, and where older adults are active participants in the city's future — not bystanders to it.

Why people will attend: Our marketing speaks directly, in participants' own language: "You belong here. You're not too old. This is for you." Every piece of outreach uses real language from real Austin adults. That authenticity, combined with trusted neighborhood locations and zero cost to attend, is our most powerful draw.
