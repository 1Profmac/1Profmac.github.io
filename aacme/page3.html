A Proven Model Built on
Three Design Principles

50+TechBridge is not a concept. It is a validated, documented program with signed attendance records, session photographs, and prior City of Austin funding.

01

Accessibility

All training is free to participants, delivered at community sites they already use — no transportation, no cost, no barriers.

02

Customization

No two programs are identical. Training content is tailored to the specific workforce, economic, and community needs of each partner site.

03

Creative Output

Participants produce real work using AI tools — writing, design, digital storytelling — culminating in a public Community Showcase.

Community Showcase — Public Arts Event

The grant year concludes with a public Community Showcase featuring participant-created digital work produced using AI tools during the program. Target: 60+ attendees. The Showcase serves as both a celebration of participant achievement and a demonstration of what becomes possible when older adults are given access to emerging creative technologies.
