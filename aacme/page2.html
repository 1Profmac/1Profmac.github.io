The Problem Runs Deeper Than Broadband

Even adults with internet access are being left behind. Existing digital literacy programs across Austin teach email and basic internet use. No program teaches AI — the technology now reshaping employment, healthcare navigation, independent living, and creative production across every sector.

LMT does not teach email. We teach AI as a creative tool — and we go to the communities no one else is reaching.

🏛

Library-Embedded Delivery

We train inside Austin Public Library branches — institutions Black and Hispanic communities already trust and use.

⛪

Faith Community Strategy

East Austin Black churches and Hispanic Catholic parishes are targeted expansion sites, reached through warm community relationships.

🌐

Spanish-Language Programming

Bilingual instructors, translated materials, and culturally competent delivery serve Austin's Hispanic 50+ population.
