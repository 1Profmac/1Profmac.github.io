Who We Are

Learn More Technologies (LMT), dba 50+TechBridge, is an Austin, Texas-based workforce development and AI education organization. We are MBE-Certified, WIOA-Eligible, and a prior City of Austin Nexus Grant recipient. Our 501(c)(3) application is in process.

We operate the only program in Austin delivering structured AI training specifically to adults 50+ — using a creative output model that treats AI as a tool for artistic expression, storytelling, and community participation.

01

We Go to Participants

Training is delivered inside libraries, senior centers, and East Austin faith community sites. No transportation barriers.

02

Creative Output Model

Participants use AI to write, design, and tell their stories — producing real work that culminates in a Community Showcase.

03

Bilingual Delivery

Spanish-language sessions with bilingual instructors and translated materials serve Austin's Hispanic 50+ community.
